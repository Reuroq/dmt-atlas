How often is each being actually reported?

Every source gives a different number, and most pages quote whichever one suits them. This page ranks the one set of figures that can honestly be ranked, shows the others beside it labelled with what they measure, and names the archetypes that no survey has ever counted.

The one rankable set — Lawrence 2022

Content analysis of 3,778 experiences drawn from 3,305 r/DMT posts (2009–2018). This is the only source that sorts each encounter into a single entity type, so it is the only one whose figures can be put in order. The six named phenotypes account for 81.6%; the remaining 18.4% was not assigned to any of them.

Why you will see different numbers everywhere else

These are not contradictions. They are different denominators, and no other page states them side by side.

“Alien”

39%Davis 2020 — share of 2,561 respondents who chose the word ‘alien’ from a descriptor list
16.3%Lawrence 2022 — share of 3,778 experiences whose entity was classified as the alien phenotype

Davis let people tick every word that fit, so his figures sum past 100%. Lawrence sorted each encounter into one bucket. Neither is wrong; they answer different questions.

Machine elves

2.9%share of one report set using the literal term
8.4%Lawrence 2022 — the whole mythological beings phenotype that contains them

The famous name is rarer than the category it belongs to. Quoting 8.4% as ‘machine elves’ inflates the icon; quoting 2.9% as the whole mythological class deflates it.

Entity encounters overall

45.5%Lawrence 2022 — share of inhaled-DMT reports containing any entity
94%Michael 2021 — share of a 36-person naturalistic field study

A self-selected archive of written trip reports and a supervised field study are not the same population. The 36-person figure is precise and tiny; the 3,778-report figure is broad and self-selected.

What this means if you are trying to place your own encounter

The single most-reported figure is not the famous one. A feminine or maternal presence leads at 24.2%; the self-transforming machine elves that gave the subject its vocabulary are a minority percept. If what you met was ordinary-shaped, wordless, or simply present, that is closer to the centre of the distribution than the icon is.

# Fourth instruction from the owner: it has to look as real as what people see

His words: "it has to be as real visually as what people see. do you understand?"

The fidelity test you built checks that the descriptors are present. That is necessary and it stays.
It is not the bar. The bar is visual realism: a person who has had this experience looks at the screen
and recognises it, the way they recognise the best replications (the top of r/replications: Symmetric
Vision, Josie Kins' DMT breakthrough and "meeting entities" videos, the replication compilations). You
know what those look like. Your current renders do not look like that. They look like a video game:
visible polygons, wireframe trees, flat-shaded colour, tiled textures, figures made of dots and
lattice. Nobody who has been there would say "that's it" to those.

What the target looks like, from the replications and the reports:
- Continuous per-pixel detail at every zoom, no polygon edges, no repeating tiles: the geometry is
  rendered by shaders (ray-marched fractals, iterated function systems, kaleidoscopic and radial
  symmetry, domain warping, feedback buffers, fractal noise), not by low-poly meshes with textures.
- Colour that is self-lit and impossibly saturated, with gradients and iridescence, chrome and jewel
  and glass materials, bloom, glow, depth haze. Nothing matte, nothing flat.
- Everything moving all the time: breathing, morphing, rotating, tessellating, zooming, flowing,
  at a pace that never lets the eye settle.
- Beings that are continuous with the geometry, jewelled and morphing, with faces and hands that
  read as faces and hands, intricate and slightly uncanny, not mascots and not point clouds.
- Density: too much to take in, at every stage after onset, with the intensity rising toward
  breakthrough and contact and loosening on the return.

A realism gate, in addition to the coverage test:
- For every stage and being, produce a fresh HIGH render and a short clip or frame sequence, look at
  it, and answer in writing: "Would someone who has had this experience recognise this, or does it read
  as a game?" Grade RECOGNISE / CLOSE / GAME, with the specific visual reasons. Anything GAME or CLOSE
  gets rebuilt. Be as hard on this as the descriptor grading.
- Generated reference images (your image generation, from the cited descriptions) may be used to
  calibrate what you are aiming at, labelled as synthesised, and never used as a claim of witness.
- Keep it running on a decent GPU in a browser, with a lower-detail mode that is still shader-based.
- Keep the Sources drawer, the honesty labels, the coverage test and the previous acceptance.

`<<WORLD_DONE>>` alone on the last line of NOTES.md only when every stage and being is RECOGNISE and
the coverage test passes.

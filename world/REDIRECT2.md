His words: "people should be seeing the patterns and fractals, EVERYTHING. this will be as close as
anyone will ever get to doing real dmt and detailed."

The structure of the journey is right. The visuals are not there yet. What is on screen now is
stylised and low-poly: flat tiled wallpaper, clean rings, cartoon beings. What the reports and the
replication community describe is the opposite of that: overwhelming, hyper-detailed, saturated,
moving geometry that fills the entire field of view and never holds still. This build is supposed
to be the closest anyone can get to the real visuals, in detail. Make it that.

What "everything" means, from the research (`data/atlas.json` geometries, `data/corpus/summary.json`
prevalence, the replications indexed in `data/corpus/depictions.json`):

  constants (tunnels, spirals, honeycomb/lattice, cobwebs), fractal lattices and jewelled tilings,
  mandalas and symmetry fields, hyperbolic/negative-curvature space, hyper-dimensional objects,
  dimensional layering and space folding, breathing and liquid surfaces, ultra-detail at every zoom,
  living circuitry and energy conduits, visible language and glyphs, embedded faces in the patterns,
  omnidirectional 360° vision, the chrysanthemum as a real folding fractal mandala, TV-static and
  signal transitions, time geometry. Prevalence in the corpus tells you how dominant each is.
- Colour and light as reported: impossibly saturated, neon-on-black and gold-on-red, chrome and
  jewel surfaces, self-illuminated, "more real than real" sharpness.
- Motion always: everything breathing, morphing, rotating, tessellating, zooming; nothing static.
- Beings that are made OF the geometry and its light (fractal humanoids, jewelled self-transforming
  forms, morphing faces), intricate and unsettling rather than cartoon mascots; the jester,
  mantis, maternal presence and elves as the reports describe them.
- Density and scale: it should feel like too much to take in, at every stage after onset.
